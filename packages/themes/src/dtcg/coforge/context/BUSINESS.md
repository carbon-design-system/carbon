# Business

**Org:** CoForge. Identity is first-party (coforge.com intent), encoded as an
overlay on Carbon — not a fork of IBM themes. **Product:** Luma Travel — a
travel product on that overlay (search, access verification, itinerary sharing,
commitment moments). Not generic enterprise ops chrome. **What the DS must
stay:** Carbon-compatible (Code Connect, vendor component names) while reading
as CoForge: bone ground, navy ink, coral used sparingly. **Constraints:** WCAG
AA. Coral on bone is 2.82:1 — large-type CTA fill only. Coral-text on bone is
the only coral legal for labels/links. **Out of scope until named:** dark
register (brand OQ-5), density registers, replacing IBM Plex maps.
